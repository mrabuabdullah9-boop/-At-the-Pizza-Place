A small green or black fruit eaten or put on pizza.

03. Grammar Capsule

Use have to and don't have to to talk about rules and things that are necessary or not necessary.

Have to

Use have to when something is necessary or it is a rule.

We always have to do our homework.
We have to be polite and on time.

Don't have to

Use don't have to when something is not necessary.

You don't have to pay for the pizzas!
We don't have to type our homework.
Remember: After have to or don't have to, always use the base form of the verb (go, pay, do, type, wear).
